Dicewars Privacy Notice

Starter notice for the Dicewars booking app.

When you make a booking, Dicewars may collect your name, email address, phone number (if provided), booking date, time, table and player count so the booking can be administered.

Booking information should only be used for operating the table-booking service, responding to customers and managing cancellations. Keep access to booking records restricted to authorised Dicewars staff.

Do not launch the production service until Dicewars has confirmed its final privacy contact details, retention period, legal basis and any third-party services used for hosting, email or analytics.
